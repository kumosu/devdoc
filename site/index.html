KUMO.SU

Bring Your Own Cloud

Your private cloud — enabling p2p applications and personal data sovereignty.

What is it?

KUMO.SU extends your smartphone with the power to run your own independent social media, sites, and p2p applications — or just cloud storage.

It replaces Google Drive, iCloud, and social media platforms by extending your digital fortress beyond your device.

Why?

  • Get rid of app vendor lock-in
  • Total privacy — server apps run in user-owned isolated environments
  • Cost efficiency — under-utilized apps cost nearly nothing
  • Same convenience for self-hosted and managed cloud options
  • App developers focus on making apps, not managing infrastructure

App Tiers

Tier I — Personal Cloud

  • Photos (Immich)
  • Music (Navidrome)
  • Password Manager (Vaultwarden)

Tier II — Fediverse / P2P

  • Messaging (Matrix / Conduit)
  • Microblogging (microblog.pub)
  • Video (PeerTube)
  • Photos (Pixelfed)
