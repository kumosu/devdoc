YouTube is great product. Their recommendation system creates great engagement and let authors reach their audience.
But there a lot of questions about the form of the product and the content. Generally, it's a [[VLOP]].

Our vision is decentralized system like PeerTube.
Each author/publisher/channel has a dedicated server. They can do any form of monetization: from ads, till micropayments in x402 spirit.

Bandwidth can be shared with supporters instead of viewers, replacing CDN.

Custom design for the channels. Custom viewer journeys can be implemented (one video is available if another has been seen).

Basically, distribution can be done better if owned by authors. [[Recommendation system]] is a separate concern.
