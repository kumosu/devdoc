What is product proposition of an online classifieds:  

* it is solving disputes/fraud/trust (at least to some degree)  
* it has all data aggregated and searchable  
  
Can such site be replaced with federation?  
Of course! I'd say the best thing that can happen with such platform is the decentralization.
So many beautiful corners will pop out.

At the most simple form it is a matter of enabling users to setup their small local shops, and let other user to find the offerings.

Search&aggregation can be as simple as apperance of the shops in search engine.

Trust/fraud can be solved by deanonimization, Cash-on-Delivery service, or trusting users to their banks, or [[Trust networks]]
