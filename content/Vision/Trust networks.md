In federation or p2p, fighting fraud and spam becomes ongoing problem to solve. It doesn't make decentralization bad, it actually gives users control of their group identity and embrace direct connections.

[[BYOC]] concept aims to aggregate users identities throughout different applications. It should be possible for applications to use users network to setup trust. This will help to spread and attribute legitimate content.

## Trust as a service

It may look at managed service provided to server apps. Using that service, the app can setup internet connections to user's friend's apps, forming overlay networks.
