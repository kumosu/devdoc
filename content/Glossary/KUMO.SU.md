
This is cloud provider implementing [[BYOC]] concept. It's purpose is to give everyone their own independent space on the internet without burden of running a server which in general is not trivial.

KUMOSU is basically a hosting provider for software that follow BYOC standard. Because of this, economy of scale comes in to power without creating a walled garden like [[VLOP]]s are.

There is no free-forever tiers. Because we respect users and don't want to monetize them in some other implicit and harmful way. KUMOSU is not social platform or something like this. We charge purely based on storage and compute used by user's applications.

One-click migration to user-owned server is promised to be there for the whole life of the project to not keep user's data hostage.
